## 3. What each knows at the cabin

These are what's in his head when the game starts. They decide which lines are honest, which are guesses, and which are reveals.

**Liam about Xiao**
- Knows: Xiao is a VP and an architect, from Facebook. Xiao messaged him once, in Year 3.
- Thinks: Xiao has had the "big shot life". Liam envies it and feels small next to it.
- Doesn't know: the panic attacks, the big firm forcing him out, Sheldon, his parents, Donna's ultimatum.

**Xiao about Liam**
- Knows: Liam has a daughter and a partner, from photos. Liam didn't answer when it mattered.
- Thinks: Liam moved on happily and Xiao wasn't needed.
- Doesn't know: Missy's illness, the money, the bouncing, Mary leaving for that weekend, Liam's dad, or that Liam took up chess.

Both of them have only seen each other's curated life. The whole game is two people swapping the highlight reel for the real thing, or choosing not to.

## 5. Proposals (confirm, cut or change each)

- **P1. Facebook irony.** In Year 3, Liam's envy comes from an outdated post, at the exact moment Xiao's life is falling apart. Liam ignored the message partly because he thought Xiao was doing better than him. At the cabin, "I thought you were living the big shot life" can land as a gut punch.

- **P2. The Year 3 message was casual on purpose.** Xiao couldn't make himself type the truth. The missed call came first, then:
  > *hey. been a while. you free to talk sometime this week?*

  This makes Liam's ignoring it understandable, which means neither of them is the villain. Liam's guilt sits less in "I abandoned him" and more in "I decided he wasn't important anymore".

- **P3. Each took up the other's thing.**
  - Xiao taught Liam chess in high school; that was how loud Liam befriended the quiet kid, and Liam always lost.
  - Liam's dad taught Liam to fish, and Liam dragged Xiao fishing at uni.
  - Liam's Year 9 chess hobby begins as games with his dad during the recovery.
  - At the cabin, Liam is better at chess than Xiao expects. That's exactly what the adaptive line "You've gotten better. Can't let you show me up now" is reacting to. The mechanic becomes story.
  - Xiao hasn't fished since uni.

- **P4. The lake is Liam's dad's spot.** Xiao picked the cabin because Liam talked about the place at uni. Liam recognises it on arrival and realises Xiao remembered. Xiao never says it out loud.

- **P5. The cabin was a uni promise.** At uni they planned "one day: a cabin, no phones, chess and fishing all weekend". That links to Liam's Year 5 "I used to want to live in one". Xiao's Year 10 message calls back to it. Draft:
  > *I booked the cabin. The one we always said we'd do. Weekend of the 14th. You don't have to come.*

- **P6. Xiao's Years 6–7, filled in.**
  - Year 6: Donna asks him to move in. He keeps his own apartment, "for work".
  - Year 7: the CEO cuts back his hours and makes Xiao vice-president.
  - Year 7: **Donna proposes, not Xiao.** He says *"yeah, sure."* In Year 9, those same two words are what break them. The phrase that made the marriage ends it.

- **P7. Present-day Donna.** Separated, not divorced. She's back in her late father's house. They still share an office of ten people, as president and vice-president, and they don't speak. Xiao hasn't told anyone.

- **P8. The graduation glance was Liam's guilt talking.** Xiao looked tired from the internship and nothing more. Liam saw "worse" because he felt bad. At the cabin, Liam's opinion of how Xiao looked can come up, and Xiao won't remember the glance at all. Or the reverse: Xiao remembers, and Liam doesn't.

- **P9. Mary sent him.** She remembers the Year 1 "what's wrong?" drive. When the message arrives in Year 10, she's the one who says something like *"Go. You've been weird about him for nine years."* Liam can admit this at the cabin.

- **P10. The unsent wedding invite (optional, heavier).** Xiao wrote Liam a Year 8 wedding invitation and never sent it. If you want a physical object, it's in his bag. I'd only use this if it doesn't crowd out P2.

## 6. How it plays at the cabin (proposal)

The game is open-ended, so story arrives as **topics that open as trust builds**, through Talk, idle messages, chess moments and quiet fishing. Your point of view decides whose secrets **you** choose to reveal; the friend's secrets surface in their lines.

**Tier 1, the highlight reel (safe)**
- Work titles
- Missy and her photos
- The belly and the beard
- Uni memories
- The song
- "How's the wife"

**Tier 2, the real version**
- Liam: the money, the second job, the car, Mary's weekend away
- Xiao: the big firm ("the quiet guy"), quitting, the CEO's death

**Tier 3, the things neither has said**
- The Year 3 message
- Sheldon, within the F7 limits
- Donna and "yeah, sure"
- Liam's disappointment in himself
- Xiao's panic attacks
- P4: Xiao remembered the lake

**Endings.** There are no good or bad endings, just how likely "next time" feels.
- Little shared: they promise to "do this again soon", and both know it's a lie. That's the default and the theme.
- Tier 3 shared on both sides: something concrete, like an invitation to meet Missy, a chess game over the phone, or "same weekend next year?"
- Lopsided, where only one opens up: a bittersweet ending somewhere in between.

## 7. Ink conventions (how the game reads the `.ink` files)

**Files** (in `public/story/`, so they ship with a build). `liam.ink` (+ `liam_inserts.ink`) is the Liam side: you play Liam, Xiao's lines arrive as bubbles. `xiao.ink` (+ `xiao_inserts.ink`) is the reverse. The game compiles them in the browser when the page loads (`src/game/story.js`, inkjs in `vendor/`), so you can edit an `.ink` file and just reload.

**Topics.** Six subjects per side, plus a seventh on Liam's side.
- You can raise three from the start. Liam side: Work, Family, The cabin. Xiao side: Work, The cabin, Uni days.
- The friend brings up the other three himself during quiet stretches (`intro`, picked at random from the ones that are ready). You answer, and from then on the topic is in your Talk list.
- Liam side only: **The little bloke** (`sheldon`, 2 layers) opens a few turns after the brother reveal (family layer 4). Layer 2 (the games, that snake game) needs version B first.
- Every return to a topic goes one layer deeper (`d_<topic>` counts them). Layer 2 needs trust 2, layer 3 needs 4, layer 4 needs 6, layer 5 needs 8, layer 6 needs 10. A few layers have extra gates: the brother's version B needs `quiet`, the voicemail payoff needs version B first, and Liam's work layer 6 (who am I) needs Donna layer 3.
- Liam's Work has six layers: 5 is the money beat (he starts to ask, "[Xiao, could you—]", and it comes out as "…Never mind. Doesn't matter."), 6 is "who am I outside work and home / I'm as lost as you".
- A topic disappears from Talk when its next layer isn't open yet (or it's shelved), and comes back once trust (or time) allows it.

**Choices are state of mind.** Options are what the character *can* say right now, and he reaches for the joke before the truth. The honest option is always offered, but it only lands once trust is high enough (`lands(n)`). Said too early, it plays a short `l<n>_soon` stitch where Xiao doesn't quite take it in, then `shelve()` undoes the layer and hides the topic for 4 turns; when it comes back, `again` has Xiao ask "did you mean that?" before the layer replays. Honest options are sticky choices (`+`) so they're still there on the replay. Xiao's options are clipped and "…" is always near. On the lake (P4) Xiao is never offered the truth.

**Tags.** `#xiao` / `#liam` say who speaks, and `#beat` is an italic stage direction. In `* [Short label] Full line #liam` the button shows the label and the bubble shows the line. A choice of `…` is silence, and the reply timer picks it. Tags go at the end of a line, after the speaker tag.
- `#key`: the choices after this line in the passage have no reply timer. On the first line of every layer 3+ and every key beat (brother, voicemail, Donna, invite, mirror, Mary, money, the little bloke).
- `#focus`: focus mode for the rest of the passage (you must answer; the camera looks across at the friend). On the brother reveal (family l4 "He passed." and l5), the voicemail (uni l3, l4), Donna's "yeah, sure" (donna l3), the invite (donna l4) and the money beat (work l5).
- `#stay` / `#ngplus`: on the output lines of the two closing choices of `story_end`. `#stay` keeps you at the lake; `#ngplus` starts New game+.

**Entry points the game calls.**
| Knot | When |
|---|---|
| `arrival` | ~1.5 s after you sit down on a new trip: chairs, rods, the board, a beer |
| `morning` | instead of `arrival` when you resume a saved trip (1–3 lines, varies by `night` and trust) |
| `talk` | the Talk button (shows only when `has_topics()` is true) |
| `idle` | quiet stretches every ~40–70 s: a new topic from the friend, or one small insert (each plays once) |
| `chess_better` | when Liam gets about 3 pawns ahead (on either side), once per trip |
| `goodnight` | when you press Quit: you turn in for the night (or take a break by day) and the trip is saved (1–3 lines, varies by `night` and trust) |
| `story_end` | once `story_done()` is true: Xiao says that's everything, then two choices tagged `#stay` / `#ngplus` |
| `leaving` | Settings → New game, the real goodbye: a few quick lines, then the menu |

**Functions the game calls.** `has_topics()` (show the Talk button), `intro_due()` (the friend has a topic to raise), `story_done()` (every layer of every topic has been played; then `has_topics()` and `intro_due()` are both false).

**Variables.** The game sets `night`, `quiet` (you're facing the water and nothing is nibbling) and `newgame_plus` (true on New game+; `arrival` adds one déjà-vu beat). The story keeps `open_liam` / `open_xiao`, `turn` (counts `talk` and `idle` passages), and per topic `wait_<topic>` / `reask_<topic>` for shelving. Endings depend mostly on how open *your* character got (Liam side: `open_liam >= 14 && open_xiao >= 14` is "really next time", `open_liam >= 7 || open_xiao >= 12` is lopsided, else the polite lie). A trip is saved on Quit and resumes where it was; New game and New game+ start the story fresh.

**Setting year: the cabin is 2026**, so uni was about 2016–17. Uni-era references have to fit that (slither.io, not Vampire Survivors). The snake game is just called "that snake game".

**Only Liam's side is playable for now.** `xiao.ink` hasn't had the round 2 changes yet.
