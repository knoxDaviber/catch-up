# Xiao Part 2: outline

**DRAFT, not canon.** Proposal for the author to edit. Built from the author's notes and ARG idea. Nothing here goes into `.ink` until confirmed.

## Premise
Xiao's story, after Liam's. Same lake, same cosy scene, but Xiao is not sleeping. The cabin weekend in part 1 was a hand held out; part 2 is what the hand was holding on to. It is likely the final story: the aftermath of Xiao. It must never shrink the theme (two people choosing to reconnect). The ARG layer only widens the world around the lake; it never overwrites what happened there.

## Tone
Darker, sadder, quiet horror. Still the same warm game: the fire is lit, the chess is still chess. The dread comes from small things being a little off, and from how tired Xiao is. No jump scares, no gore. Grief and exhaustion are the monsters. Sheldon's death stays at "he passed" plus how Xiao feels, per canon; anything beyond that is ARG-reserved and not written in this game.

## Beat list (proposed)
1. **Arrival.** Xiao looks like he has not slept since part 1. Dry and clipped, now also flat. The friend is kind and visibly worried.
2. **Sleep.** Going to bed is the hard part. Quit-for-the-night lines change: "not tonight".
3. **First wrongness.** Something minor in the scene (see below). Xiao does not mention it.
4. **Donna.** The separation and the sleeplessness are linked. He does not say how much.
5. **The old pattern.** It happened after Sheldon too. One sentence, then he changes the subject.
6. **The visitors.** Over several nights the wrongness moves closer to the water: a figure on the far shore, a second rod in the water that nobody cast. Never shown clearly. (See open question on who they are.)
7. **The hard night.** The friend asks "when did you last sleep". Xiao chooses whether to tell him anything.
8. **Morning.** Nothing is solved. Something small and human is kept (he stays, makes breakfast, does not try to fix it). Warm-bittersweet, not tidy.

## How the horror shows in the cosy scene
- Reflections lag half a second; the second chair is occupied in the corner of the eye and empty when looked at.
- The radio drifts to a station that is almost a song; smoke from the fire leans the wrong way.
- The float sits still on windless nights, then moves against the current.
- Chess: the friend's piece is sometimes already moved when Xiao looks back. Never confirmed or explained.
- The clock skips an hour at night; lines mention being "up" for days.
- Sound: ambient beds thin out, birdsong cuts mid-phrase. Silence is the scare.
- Everything is deniable as exhaustion. The player is never told it is real.

## How harder fishing ties to the theme
Fishing is Xiao's way of staying present. When sleep-deprived, fish are harder, not meaner: longer waits, heavier line, bites in the wrong rhythm, and a fight minigame where the reel slips as if the hand is shaky. Rested days (after a good talk, or when the friend stays up with him) ease it back. The difficulty is Xiao's state made playable and rewards patience and company, not skill. Some fish only bite at night, when the scene is at its worst.
